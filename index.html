AEPS / Mini Bank

Aadhaar based banking — no bank queue needed

Using the AEPS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System) counter at Computer Care, you can withdraw cash, check your balance and transfer money with just your Aadhaar number and fingerprint. All transactions are done on a government-approved biometric device and a printed receipt is given for every transaction.

Cash WithdrawalTake out cash from your Aadhaar-linked bank account.
Balance EnquiryKnow your account balance instantly.
Mini StatementLast transactions printed for your records.
Aadhaar PayPay merchants directly with your fingerprint.
Money TransferSend money to any bank account.
Recharge & BillsMobile, DTH recharge and utility bill payments.
What to bring: your Aadhaar number and the same finger you registered with your bank. Keep your registered mobile handy — a receipt is given for every transaction.
